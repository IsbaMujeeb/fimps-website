Got Questions?

We’ve Got Answers

Are FIMPS Beauty products cruelty-free and organic?
Yes, absolutely! All our formulations are 100% cruelty-free, non-toxic, and crafted with high-quality organic ingredients designed to provide safe, nourishing results for all skin types.
How can I track my order?
Once your order is shipped, you will receive a confirmation email containing your tracking number and a link to check your delivery status in real-time.
What is your return and exchange policy?
We offer a hassle-free 14-day return and exchange policy for unused or unopened items in their original packaging. Please reach out to our support team to start a return.
Do you offer cash on delivery (COD)?
Yes, Cash on Delivery is available for all eligible local orders. You can also pay securely online via credit or debit cards during checkout.
How do I know which skincare product suits my skin type?
Each product page includes detailed information regarding active ingredients, skin types, and benefits. If you need personalized recommendations, feel free to contact us via our Contact Us page!
